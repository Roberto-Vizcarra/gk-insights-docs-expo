<kbd>Last updated: September 2026</kbd>

GitKraken Insights tracks 21 metrics across five families. Each family measures a different dimension of your team's AI adoption and delivery health.

| Family | What it measures |
| --- | --- |
| [Adoption & Agentic](/gk-insights/ai-adoption-agentic-metrics) | How broadly and autonomously your team uses AI |
| [Output & Throughput](/gk-insights/ai-adoption-output-metrics) | What your team ships — volume, effort, and velocity |
| [Flow & Cycle Time](/gk-insights/ai-adoption-flow-metrics) | How fast work moves from commit to merge |
| [DORA & Quality](/gk-insights/ai-adoption-dora-metrics) | The four DORA metrics plus quality signals |
| [AI Impact & Cost](/gk-insights/ai-adoption-impact-cost-metrics) | What AI is delivering in time and dollars |
